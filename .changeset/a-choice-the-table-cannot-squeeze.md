Keep a choice in a table cell as wide as its longest option.

A select in a cell is drawn at a percentage width, and at a percentage width a select counts for nothing when a table works out how narrow a column may go. On a full table the column holding one was squeezed until "Member" read "Mer" beside its chevron. The select's own width, the longest choice it holds, is now the least the column gives it.
