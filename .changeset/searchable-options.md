Answer `Select.searchable()` on the server.

`optionsLimit` makes a relationship select's list a window, and until now there
was no way to reach past it. A new route runs a term against the label field
the declaration named — never against a field that did not say it could be
searched, and never in a tree resolved from what arrived.
