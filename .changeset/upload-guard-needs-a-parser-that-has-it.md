Ask for the parser the upload guard needs. The route refuses a field name carrying brackets, and it does that by asking multer for a limit — but an option a parser does not know is ignored rather than refused, so on an older one the guard is a no-op with nothing to say so. `@nestjs/platform-express` pins its parser exactly: 11.0.0 pins multer 1.4.5-lts.1, which has no such option, and the limit only begins to be read at 11.1.28. The peer range said `^11.0.0`, so the panel advertised installations where its own refusal quietly does nothing. It now says `^11.2.6`, which is the version whose parser this repository actually runs in CI — a floor at 11.1.28 would make the guard work and still advertise a parser nobody here tests, and the parser is the load-bearing part now. It also carries the four advisories multer closed in 2.3.0, one of which the guard cannot close because it is in the parsing itself. `@nestjs/common` and `@nestjs/core` stay at `^11.0.0`: nothing in this package depends on a minor of either, and a floor without a reason is a floor somebody has to argue with later. Breaking under the rule that a minor before 1.0 is breaking: an application on `@nestjs/platform-express` 11.0 or 11.1 installed yesterday and does not today. The upgrade is `pnpm up "@nestjs/*@^11.2.6"`, and on the same line as this release rather than as a separate migration.
