Add `.extraAttributes()` on a column, for whoever reads the page from outside it: a tour that points at a column, a test that finds one, a screen reader that needs a word the value does not carry. Held to the rule a control's own attributes are held to, by the same expression asked of the same names, so an attribute allowed on a control and refused on a cell cannot come about by two lists drifting. `data-label` is refused with them, and not as an oversight: it is how a row becomes a stack on a narrow screen, and a column that took that name would be unlabelled exactly where the label is the only thing naming it. Refused at boot and ignored by the renderer, because a column that is fine on a laptop and unlabelled on a phone is a bug nobody reproduces.
